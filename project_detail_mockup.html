RESIDENTIAL—KOLAR, BHOPAL—2026

Residence 04

The brief

A steeply sloped half-acre plot ruled out a conventional single-level layout — the house had to step down with the land, not fight it.

The clients wanted three generations under one roof without shared walls between them. The response splits the program across three stepped volumes connected by a covered walkway, each with its own entrance, light, and outlook — private when needed, together when it matters.

Approach

Working with the slope

Rather than cutting and filling to flatten the plot, the three volumes step down at three different levels, cutting excavation by roughly a third.

Approach

Local stone, re-read

Coursed sandstone from a quarry 40km away is used on the plinth and party walls, left unpolished so it weathers the way the surrounding hillside does.

Approach

One shared spine

A covered walkway ties the three households together without ever forcing them through each other's spaces — the one deliberate compromise on privacy.
